# BT Retail Analytics – Internship Projects

Data analysis projects developed during a 3-week internship at **Banca Transilvania**, Retail Department, as part of the EU-funded project *"Experiențele bune ne țin împreună"* (MySMIS 311108).

**Period:** April – May 2026  
**Location:** BT Headquarters, Cluj-Napoca  
**Department:** Retail – Data & Modeling  

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- **Qlik** – data modeling and reporting
- **SQL** – data extraction and querying
- **Power BI** – data visualization
- **Excel** – statistical analysis, Chi-Square testing

---

## 📁 Projects

### 1. Customer Churn Prediction *(Team Project)*
Predictive risk-scoring model built on the Churn_Modelling dataset to identify clients at risk of leaving the bank. Achieved **78% accuracy** classifying clients into High / Medium / Low risk segments.

### 2. BT Virtual Card Analysis *(Individual Project)*
Competitive benchmarking of BT's Virtual Card product against Revolut, BCR, and Alpha Bank, with a UX improvement proposal for a Single-Use Virtual Card feature.  
🏆 **Award winner** at the program's closing competition.

### 3. Financial Inclusion in Romania *(Individual Project)*
Data-driven study on banking inclusion trends in Romania (2025–2026), analyzing barriers to access across income, education, and geographic segments, visualized in Power BI.

---

## ⚠️ Note
All datasets used are either publicly available or anonymized. No real client data from Banca Transilvania is included in this repository.
